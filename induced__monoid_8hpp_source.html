13// EVIDENCE, NOT PROPOSED WORDING. These are two monoids the library's own
14// typeclass instances induce, not a facility D3200R0 proposes: a Monad
15// induces a monoid on its Kleisli arrows, and an Applicative induces a
16// monoid on its context lifted from a monoid on the element. Both are
17// monoid *instances* over bare value types -- an erased arrow type, and the
18// context itself -- and neither registers anything for any raw carrier; see
19// docs/decisions.md#monoid-selection. This header exists to show the
20// mechanism carries the induction without strain, and to record, once, the
21// one thing worth not rediscovering: the categorical statement "a monad is
22// a monoid in the category of endofunctors" is inexpressible at `Monoid<T>`,
23// whose tensor is a product and whose carrier is a type, not a type
24// constructor. The Kleisli endomorphism monoid below is the value-level
25// statement that survives that gap -- evidence of the theorem, not the
26// theorem itself.
