Detailed Description

template<typename TREE>
class beman::typeclass_tree_adapters::FingerTreePart< TREE >

One part of a finger tree: the tree at a spine level, or an element node.

This is the handle a projection hands out and the piece an embedding assembles.

A part owns a share of the immutable structure it denotes. Copying one is O(1) and copies no payload, and a part is valid for as long as it exists.
