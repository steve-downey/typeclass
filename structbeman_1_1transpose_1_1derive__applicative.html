Detailed Description

template<class Impl>
struct beman::transpose::derive_applicative< Impl >

CRTP base for Applicative instances.

Impl must provide pure(value) and either the n-ary invoke(f, args_in_context...) or the one-step ap(f_in_context, arg_in_context); the base derives the missing one and every other operation. invoke is the user-facing interface; ap is the classic basis. A Map may re-expose Impl::invoke with a using-declaration, as the library's own instances do, but it need not: the base's invoke is constrained on whichever basis Impl supplies, so leaving the using-declaration out never turns a probe of invoke into a hard error. Re-exposing is an optimization that skips the base's dispatch.
