Detailed Description

template<class T>
struct beman::transpose::min_monoid< T >

Minimum monoid on T: combine takes the smaller of the two.

The identity is the saturating upper bound of T – positive infinity where T has one, std::numeric_limits<T>::max() otherwise – for the same reasons max_monoid gives.
