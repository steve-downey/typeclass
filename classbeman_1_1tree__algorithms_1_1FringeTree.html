Detailed Description

template<class T>
class beman::tree_algorithms::FringeTree< T >

Persistent balanced binary tree representing a sequence of leaf values.

Empty, a single Leaf, or a Branch of two subtrees; values live exclusively at leaves; branches carry a cached measure (leaf count). Subtrees are held by shared_ptr, so copies are cheap and concatenation shares structure.
