Detailed Description

template<class T>
struct beman::transpose::sender< T >

A minimal deferred (lazy) value: the wrapped computation runs on get().

The work may be a move-only callable – one that owns a std::unique_ptr, say. std::function needs a copyable target, so a move-only one is boxed in a shared_ptr and the box is what run holds; copies of the sender share that one piece of work. A copyable callable is stored directly.
