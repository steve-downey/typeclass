Look up the transpose::functor instance for the concrete layer type of layer and apply its fmap.

This is the single point where the verb bodies bridge into transpose::functor dispatch: instead of threading an explicit fmap_fn through every recursive call, they call layer_fmap(fn, layer) and the lookup keyed on std::remove_cvref_t<Layer> finds the right fmap.
