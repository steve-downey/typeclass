Detailed Description

template<typename TREE>
class beman::fingertree::tree::FingerTreeLevel< TREE >

The tree at one level of a finger tree's spine, in its real form: Empty, Single, or Deep with a left digit, the next level, and a right digit.

Level 0 is the sequence itself; the nodes held at level d have height d. Owns a share of the immutable structure.
