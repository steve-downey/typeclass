Detailed Description

Projection for the direct verbs: exposes one layer of a BinaryTree, children as raw non-owning pointers read from the shared_ptr spine in place — no conversion, no structure copied beyond one layer.

The pointer handles are complete types, so their slots are inline: the projected layer allocates nothing.
