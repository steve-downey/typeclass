Detailed Description

Projection for fold_with: exposes one layer of a finger tree as it is stored.

Positions are parts; a Leaf shares its payload. Nothing is copied except reference counts.

The pointer overload is the root of a fold in the tree's own representation, the shape tree_algorithms::project registrations take.
