Detailed Description

Folds one FingerTreeF layer elementwise.

The contractual order is the sequence order: a Deep combines its left digit, its spine, then its right digit, each left to right, and a node combines its children left to right. A Leaf is one mapped payload. Only an Empty layer yields the identity; no other form combines with it.
